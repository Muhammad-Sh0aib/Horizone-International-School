ABOUT HORIZON

More Than a School. A Community Where Potential Takes Shape.

Horizon International School is committed to creating an environment where students are challenged to think deeply, encouraged to explore boldly and supported as they grow into confident, responsible individuals.

Discover Our Approach Meet Our Leadership

WHO WE ARE

A Learning Community Built Around Every Student

At Horizon International School, we believe every student brings unique strengths, interests and aspirations to the classroom. Our role is to create the environment, guidance and opportunities that allow those qualities to grow.

Through purposeful teaching, meaningful experiences and a strong sense of community, we help students develop the knowledge, confidence and character they need to navigate an increasingly connected world.

OUR JOURNEY

Our Journey

Our story has been shaped by a commitment to thoughtful education, strong relationships and a belief that schools should evolve with the needs of their students and communities.

2001

The Beginning

Horizon International School opens its doors with a vision to create a learning environment centered on academic foundations and individual potential.

2008

Growing With Our Community

The school expands its academic programs and introduces new extracurricular opportunities for students.

2015

A New Era of Learning

Technology-enhanced classrooms, updated laboratories and collaborative learning spaces become part of the school's educational environment.

2021

Preparing Students for Tomorrow

The school strengthens its focus on digital literacy, critical thinking, leadership and interdisciplinary learning.

2026

Looking Ahead

Horizon continues to evolve while remaining committed to its core purpose: helping students learn with purpose, grow with confidence and contribute with character.

OUR MISSION

Mission

MESSAGE FROM THE PRINCIPAL

Every Student Deserves the Opportunity to Discover What They Can Become

At Horizon International School, we see education as a partnership between students, educators and families. Our responsibility is not simply to prepare students for examinations, but to help them become thoughtful learners, confident communicators and responsible members of society.

We want every student who walks through our doors to feel known, supported and encouraged to aim higher. Through strong teaching, meaningful relationships and opportunities beyond the classroom, we strive to create an environment where curiosity is welcomed and potential can flourish.
