Learning That Builds Knowledge, Character and Confidence

Explore Programs View Curriculum

OUR APPROACH

Strong Foundations. Meaningful Learning.

At Horizon International School, academic success begins with a strong foundation. We provide structured learning experiences while encouraging students to question, explore, collaborate and apply their knowledge in meaningful ways.

Our teachers combine clear instruction with discussion, practical activities, projects and opportunities for independent thinking so that students understand not only what they are learning, but why it matters.

Learning with purpose
Growing with confidence

PROGRESSION

A Learning Journey for Every Stage

Students progress through carefully structured stages designed to meet their changing academic, social and personal needs.

Early Years

Ages 3–5

Building curiosity, communication, independence and early learning foundations through purposeful play and guided exploration.

Primary School

Grades 1–5

Developing literacy, numeracy, scientific thinking and a love of learning with strong foundational skills.

Middle School

Grades 6–8

Strengthening subject knowledge, critical thinking, collaboration and independent learning with broader academic exploration.

Secondary School

Grades 9–10

Deepening academic understanding while developing responsibility, confidence and examination readiness.

Higher Secondary

Grades 11–12

Preparing students for higher education, professional pathways and increasingly independent learning.

OUR CURRICULUM

More Than Memorization

We believe education should help students understand ideas, connect knowledge and use what they learn. Our curriculum balances academic rigor with opportunities for creativity, collaboration, investigation and real-world application.

LEARNING AREAS

Core Learning Areas

Students develop knowledge across a broad range of disciplines while discovering the subjects and interests that inspire them.

METHODOLOGY

How Learning Happens

Our classrooms are designed to encourage participation, curiosity and thoughtful exploration.

Inquiry-Based Learning

Students ask questions, investigate ideas and build understanding through exploration and discovery.

Collaborative Learning

Students learn through teamwork, discussion and shared problem solving with peers and teachers.

Project-Based Learning

Students apply knowledge to meaningful projects and practical challenges that demonstrate real-world applications.

Technology-Enhanced Learning

Digital tools support research, creativity, communication and learning in purposeful and intentional ways.

Experiential Learning

Students connect classroom concepts with activities, experiments and real-world experiences.

Independent Learning

Students gradually develop confidence, organization and responsibility for their own educational progress.

STUDENT PROGRESS

Measuring Progress, Supporting Growth

Assessment at Horizon is used not simply to measure results, but to understand how students are progressing and where they can improve.

1

Understand

Teachers identify strengths and areas that need attention.

2

Support

Students receive guidance, feedback and opportunities to improve.

3

Progress

Learning goals are reviewed and students own their development.

Assessment Methods

  • Classroom activities
  • Projects and presentations
  • Practical work
  • Written assessments
  • Term examinations
  • Teacher feedback and observation
