REQUIREMENTS

Admission Requirements

Requirements may vary depending on the student's age, grade and previous educational background.

General Requirements

  • Appropriate age for the requested grade
  • Previous academic record where applicable
  • Student and parent/guardian information
  • Successful completion of the school's admission process
  • Availability of a place in the requested grade

For New Students

  • Previous school information
  • Academic records
  • Student identification information
  • Parent/guardian contact details

For Transfer Students

  • Transfer documentation where applicable
  • Previous academic records
  • Current grade information
  • Relevant school records

Admission decisions are based on available places, age/grade suitability, required documentation and the overall admission process.

DOCUMENTS

Documents You May Need

Student Information

  • Birth certificate or official birth record
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Previous school records where applicable

Parent / Guardian Information

  • Parent or guardian identification
  • Contact information
  • Residential address

Academic Records

  • Previous report cards
  • Transfer certificate where applicable
  • Relevant assessment records

Note: Document requirements may vary by grade and applicant circumstances. The admissions office will confirm the documents required for each application.
