THE HORIZON CAMPUS

Designed Around the Student Experience

Our campus brings learning, creativity, collaboration, physical activity, technology and wellbeing into one connected school community. Different spaces give students the right setting to focus, make, move, share ideas and spend time together.

From purposeful classrooms to welcoming shared areas, the environment is planned around the changing needs of students as they move through each stage of their school journey.

A Quiet Space for Curiosity

The library and learning resource centre offers a change of pace: a place to follow a question, read for enjoyment, work independently or gather material for a project. It supports both guided classroom learning and the habit of exploring an idea a little further.

Students can use the space for quiet study, reading and research, with print and digital resources supporting different learning needs.

SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY

Learning Through Discovery

From laboratory investigations to computing and digital projects, specialist learning spaces help students develop questions, test ideas and make thoughtful connections. Practical work, research and project-based learning invite students to take an active role in understanding how things work.

Teachers guide students to use tools carefully, consider evidence and share what they discover—building habits that support learning well beyond a single subject.

CAMPUS QUESTIONS

Plan Your Visit With Confidence

Some helpful details for families exploring the Horizon campus.

The campus includes everyday learning spaces, specialist environments for practical and creative work, library resources, areas for sport and outdoor activity, student common areas and spaces for support.

Prospective families can contact the admissions office to ask about arranging a visit in line with the school's visitor arrangements.

Learning environments and activities are planned with students' developmental and academic needs in mind. Staff guide students in using spaces appropriately for their age and activity.

Flexible spaces can support sports, arts, clubs, presentations, events and a range of student activities alongside the academic day.

Contact the admissions team to ask questions or discuss a campus visit. They can help you learn more about the school environment and student experience.
