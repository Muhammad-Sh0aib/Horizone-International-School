LIFE AT HORIZON

Every Event Has a Purpose

At Horizon International School, events are not merely dates on a calendar. Each assembly, exhibition, tournament, and festival is intentionally designed to nurture vital life skills: learning, collaboration, creativity, leadership, celebration, confidence, and community connection.

Whether presenting original scientific inquiry, performing under auditorium stage lights, competing on athletic fields, or welcoming families to campus, students take ownership of experiences that shape their character and voice.

Experiences that connect classroom learning with the wider world, practical inquiry, and authentic audiences.

Participate

Connect

ACROSS THE YEAR

A School Year Full of Experiences

From welcoming first days to celebratory year-end milestones, each academic term brings a purposeful rhythm of events and shared discoveries.

Term 1 (Autumn)

Beginnings & Discovery

Setting foundations, building friendships, and igniting curiosity.

Welcome orientations, new student inductions, student club sign-ups, introductory academic showcases, foundational house gatherings, and our annual Learning & Creativity Showcase.

Term 2 (Winter)

Inquiry & Expression

Deepening scholarship, collaborative teamwork, and competition.

Annual Athletics Championship, STEM and robotics workshops, winter musical concerts, inter-house debates, mathematics challenges, and mid-year parent-teacher academic dialogues.

Term 3 (Spring & Summer)

Achievement & Celebration

Reflecting on growth, sharing creations, and celebrating progress.

Spring visual arts exhibitions, literature forums, theatre and performing arts evenings, community service eco-days, transition ceremonies, and year-end commendation assemblies.

STUDENT OWNERSHIP

Students Don't Just Attend. They Participate.

Every event provides real opportunities for students to organize, lead, present, perform, collaborate, and support peers.

01

Discover

Students identify questions, explore artistic forms, develop fitness goals, or discover issues that matter to them.

02

Prepare

Under mentorship from teachers, students research, rehearse, build prototypes, and organize roles in team settings.

03

Participate

On event day, students step forward to speak, compete, perform, manage logistics, and share ideas with authentic audiences.

04

Reflect

Learners review audience feedback, celebrate growth with classmates, and apply new confidence to their everyday studies.
